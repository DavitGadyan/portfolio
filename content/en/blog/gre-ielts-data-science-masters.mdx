faq:
  - q: What exams do you need for a data science master's abroad?
    a: >-
      Most international programmes require the GRE (general aptitude, with a
      quantitative section) and a proof of English proficiency such as IELTS or
      TOEFL. Specific score requirements vary by school and programme.
  - q: Is GRE preparation useful beyond admissions?
    a: >-
      The quantitative section covers algebra, geometry, probability and data
      interpretation — the same foundations that machine learning is built on.
      Preparing for it doubles as a structured revision of the maths a data
      scientist uses.
