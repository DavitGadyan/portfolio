### Received Expert Vetted status on popular platform

After 4 years of working on Upwork with 100+ clients with over 6000 hours, I have received the highest rank on Upwork. Only the top 1% of talent on Upwork qualifies for Expert-Vetted Talent status, and only those in select categories are eligible https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049625454-Expert-Vetted-Talent. 

This achievement came true thanks to hard work and diligence. Seeing the results of my work I just got inspired to work harder and achieve more in this life.
