This site belongs to a freelance engineer, not a company. It collects very little personal data, and this page explains what, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish data-protection law.

## Who is responsible

## What data is processed, and why

### Contact form

When you send a message, I receive your **name, email address, message, the language of the page you used** and, only if you accepted analytics cookies and arrived through a Google ad, the **ad click id** of that visit. The form is delivered by **Formspree, Inc.** (USA), which stores the submission so that it can be forwarded to me by email.

- Purpose: answering your enquiry and, if you ask for one, preparing a proposal.
- Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and my legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to me (art. 6(1)(f)).
- Retention: messages are kept for as long as the conversation or project is active and deleted no later than 24 months after the last exchange, unless a contract requires longer retention.

### Booking a call

If you book a call, you enter your details directly into **Calendly** (Calendly LLC, USA), which shares the booking with me and creates a calendar event and a video-call link. Calendly's own privacy policy applies to that data. The calendar is loaded only after you press the booking button.

### Analytics

Only if you accept analytics cookies, **Google Analytics 4** (Google Ireland Ltd.) records page views, clicks on contact links and form submissions, together with technical data such as browser type, approximate location derived from your IP address (which Google does not store) and a random cookie id. I use the aggregated reports to see which pages are useful and whether enquiries actually arrive.

- Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time through **Manage cookies** in the footer.
- Retention: event data is kept by Google for 14 months, aggregated reports for longer.

### Hosting

The site is static and hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like every web server, GitHub may log your IP address and the pages requested for security purposes. I have no access to individual logs.

## Who receives data and where it goes

Data is shared only with the service providers named above, each acting under its own privacy policy and a data-processing agreement. Google, GitHub and Calendly are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; where a provider is not, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. I never sell personal data and I do not use it for profiling or automated decisions.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me to:

- tell you what data I hold about you (**access**),
- **correct** inaccurate data,
- **delete** your data ("right to be forgotten"),
- **restrict** or **object** to processing,
- receive your data in a portable format (**portability**),
- **withdraw consent** for analytics at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Write to [david.gadyan@bse.eu](mailto:david.gadyan@bse.eu); I reply within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Authority ([AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/)) or the authority in your own EU country.

This site is not aimed at children under 16 and I do not knowingly collect their data.
