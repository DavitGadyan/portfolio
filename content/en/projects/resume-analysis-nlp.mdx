## The problem

The client had two sets of resumes, high-value prospect candidates and low-value ones, and wanted to know what actually separates them. The differences were not obvious on a read-through, so the brief was to apply NLP and AI tooling to find the hidden patterns, similarities and differences between the two groups and, ideally, to predict which group a new CV belongs to.

## What I built

- Exploratory data analysis over both groups as the first step, to identify relationships and patterns in the text before touching a model.
- Feature engineering driven by those EDA insights, turning the CV text into signals the model could learn from.
- A classification model built with NLP and LLM (ChatGPT) tooling that separates high-value from low-value candidates.
- Parameter tuning against the labelled set to reach high accuracy.

## Outcome

The client provided a labelled set of around 30 CVs, which made the EDA and modelling far more reliable than an unlabelled corpus would have. The insights from that analysis powered the feature engineering and tuning, and the resulting model separates the two candidate groups with high accuracy, giving the client an explicit picture of what distinguishes them.
