## The problem

Charisma is a set of trainable behaviours, listening and building on what the other person said, and real conversations are unscripted. The only honest practice is out loud, unrehearsed and with feedback, which is what ImprovTalk is for. The build had two product problems to solve first. Practice, Simulate, Learn, History and Custom Practice had accumulated as separate features, while what users want is one path: set up, talk, get scored, look back, and see progress. And the visual language had drifted: a navy theme, six iOS system colours on the Home tiles, hand-typed reds and a brand gradient on every button, with nothing deciding between them.

## What I built

- **Scenes and live personas.** Pick a real-world scene, a café, a bar, the beach, a gym, a festival, campus or the street, hold one button and talk. The AI persona answers aloud in real time over OpenAI real-time voice and WebRTC; there is no typing.
- **Charisma Score with line-level feedback.** When the conversation ends you get what worked, what to work on and how you sounded: pace, pauses, intonation and fillers, measured from your own voice rather than guessed. The score weighs fluency, confidence and improvisation.
- **Simulate.** Multi-round scenarios against different personality blends, with a verdict after each run.
- **Learn and Custom Practice.** 104 techniques and 27 catalogs of things to talk about; Custom Practice builds a persona from a description or from a public Instagram profile.
- **Real Talk.** Upload a recording or describe a conversation you had and get a 0–100 grade, a transcript and advice on openers and next steps.
- **Plans and languages.** Free, Pro and Max on Apple In-App Purchase and Stripe, in English, Spanish and Russian.

## A design system the build enforces

- **One journey.** Four surfaces, Home, Practice, Learn and History, and a single session path: setup, live voice call, score, history.
- **A palette you cannot leave.** The five brand colours plus the four an app needs, one accent and four data colours, Satoshi, and a 30 KB Material Symbols subset so the app draws exactly the glyphs the mockups were drawn with. A CI check fails the build on any colour literal outside the token file.
- **Three versions, one decision.** Flat violet; violet with the gradient kept in two places; violet plus a signal colour. The same screens each time, so the choice was between systems, not aesthetics. The gradient now lives on Hold-to-speak and the intro only, which makes it the signature rather than a button style.
- **One flexible element.** On the live screen only the portrait flexes and everything under it has a fixed height, so the controls never leave the screen on any phone.
- **Motion as feedback.** Press response with haptics, a breathing hold-to-speak disc, a counting score and staggered entrances, all off under Reduce Motion.

## How it was delivered

React Native with Expo on the New Architecture for iOS and Android; FastAPI with Postgres and Redis behind it; OpenAI real-time voice over WebRTC for the live persona; a Playwright-based profile reader for Custom Practice; Apple In-App Purchase and Stripe for the plans; Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring. Voice recordings are used only to score sessions, and the app does not access contacts, photos or location and shows no ads.

## Marketing: generative AI video ads

The same toolchain markets the app. A pipeline on the Higgsfield API, with Claude driving the prompts and creative direction for each customer segment and platform, produces tailored variants for Instagram, Meta, TikTok and YouTube from one brief. The same brief was run through Seedance and Kling 2.6 Pro so the difference is visible before a campaign commits to one model; both outputs are below and the pipeline code is on GitHub.

## Outcome

A coherent, token-driven UI on iOS and Android with light and dark themes, a three-plan paywall and a live-session layout that fits every phone. The app is in TestFlight with the App Store release in progress, and its ads are generated rather than produced by hand.
