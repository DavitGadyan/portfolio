## The problem

The client had a hunch about the stock market: that tickers with similar names move together, and that a price change in one shows up in the others. He wanted to know whether the effect was real before building anything on top of it. Because the idea was his, the exact formulation and the tickers involved stay confidential; what I can describe is the shape of the work.

## What I built

- A clean dataset of market price data for the groups of similarly named tickers the client was interested in.
- A formal statement of the client's idea as a testable hypothesis, so the answer would be a statistical result rather than an impression taken from a chart.
- Time-series analysis in Python of how price changes in one ticker relate to price changes in its similarly named counterparts.
- A short write-up of the method and the evidence behind the result.

## Outcome

The analysis found that there is indeed a measurable relationship between price changes in similarly named tickers. The client received the statistical evidence behind that conclusion, which is what he needed to decide whether the idea was worth pursuing further.
